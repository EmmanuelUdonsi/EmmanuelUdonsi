# About Me
My academic background is in Human Physiology, where I developed an appreciation for structured thinking, research, and evidence-based reasoning. My transition into Data Analytics has allowed me to apply that analytical mindset to business problems and real-world datasets.

I'm particularly interested in exploring how data can improve business performance, operational efficiency, and strategic decision-making.

My current areas of focus include:

Data Analysis: Exploring datasets, identifying patterns, investigating trends, and extracting actionable insights.

Business Intelligence: Transforming raw data into meaningful information that supports better business decisions.

Supply Chain Analytics: Investigating inventory performance, supplier reliability, procurement efficiency, and logistics operations.

Statistical Analysis: Applying statistical concepts and analytical methods to understand relationships, test assumptions, and interpret data.

I enjoy approaching problems with curiosity, questioning assumptions, and looking beyond surface-level results to understand what the data actually reveals.
